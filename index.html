// 03 — How it works

The methodology

A reproducible pipeline any coffee shop owner could run on their own POS export.

STEP 01
Aggregate per customer

Roll 57K transactions up to recency, frequency & monetary value for each of the 800 customers.

STEP 02
RFM scoring

Each metric scored 1–5 using pd.qcut quintiles. A perfect customer scores 15/15.

STEP 03
K-Means clustering

Standardize features, then KMeans(k=5) groups customers by genuine behavioral similarity.

STEP 04
Label & act

Rank clusters by RFM, map to named segments, attach a concrete marketing action to each.
